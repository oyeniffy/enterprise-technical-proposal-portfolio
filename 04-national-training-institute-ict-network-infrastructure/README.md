## PART 3 – TECHNICAL PROPOSAL

### 3.1  Understanding of the Project and Requirements

[Client]'s mandate under the “Enhancing Vocational Training Delivery in the Nigerian Power Sector” project is to modernize its training delivery capability across its Headquarters in Abuja and eight Regional Training Centres nationwide. This requires a unified, secure, high-performance ICT network capable of supporting live and recorded technical training, video conferencing, simulation platforms, enterprise applications, and reliable staff/student connectivity – today and as the Institute scales.
We understand the network must, at minimum:
- Support 1,000 concurrent users, scalable to 4,000 concurrent users, without core redesign;
- Interconnect HQ and all 8 RTCs over an encrypted, centrally-managed SD-WAN overlay with automatic switchover between multiple backup links;
- Provide training-video assurance (low latency, no freezing) across the WAN;
- Enforce access and bandwidth control, including unrestricted bandwidth for VIP users and controlled bandwidth for standard users;
- Be managed from a single, unified LAN-WAN-WLAN software platform;
- Deliver full Wi-Fi 7 coverage at every training centre, engineered from predictive RF simulation so that all key nodes are covered before physical deployment;
- Use permanent/perpetual licensing on the SD-WAN and Network Management System to avoid service interruption from licence lapses; and
- Be delivered, tested, documented, and handed over within the Contract Period, with training for [Client]'s own ICT staff and a minimum three-year warranty and support regime.
This Technical Proposal, together with the design materials in Annexure D (Huawei Network Architecture for [Client]) and the diagrams reproduced in Section 3.3 below, demonstrates our understanding of – and full compliance with – these requirements.

### 3.2  Proposed Solution Overview

Our proposed architecture is a resilient, hierarchical, centrally-managed network built entirely on Huawei's enterprise campus and WAN portfolio, structured in four logical layers as illustrated below: an Egress/Internet layer with automated SD-WAN deployment and intelligent O&M; a Core layer providing high-speed switching and firewall protection at HQ; an Aggregation/Distribution layer extending services to each facility; and an Access layer delivering wired and Wi-Fi 7 wireless connectivity to end users and IoT devices.

#### 3.3.6  Wireless Network Architecture (Diagram 6 of 8 – Mandatory)

Wireless coverage at HQ and every RTC is delivered on Huawei AirEngine 5776-26 Wi-Fi 7 (IEEE 802.11be) access points, centrally managed via a Huawei AC6508 Wireless Access Controller located at HQ, fully integrated with the wired LAN and iMaster NCE-Campus management plane. Key differentiators of the proposed wireless solution, directly addressing [Client]'s requirements for high-density training environments, are:
- 6 spatial streams (2×2 @2.4GHz + 4×4 @5GHz), device rate up to 6.45 Gbps per AP – exceeding the required ≥5 Gbps aggregate throughput and ≥200 concurrent clients per AP.
- Converged scheduling (MU-MIMO + OFDMA + MRU across spatial, frequency, time and application domains), delivering zero-freeze performance for up to 30 concurrent 4K video streams or 120 concurrent 1080p streams per AP – 20% ahead of industry average (independently verified by Tolly Group).
- Multi-Link Operation (MLO) in high-reliability mode, reducing average latency to <20ms (P99 <50ms) even in high-interference environments – critical for latency-sensitive remote-desktop and simulation training.
- VIP Lane technology, giving [Client] the ability to designate VIP users (e.g., instructors, management) for guaranteed, unrestricted, preferential bandwidth (busy-time latency <50ms vs. >200ms industry-standard), directly satisfying the requirement that VIP-user bandwidth is not limited while standard staff bandwidth is controlled.
- Wi-Fi Shield technology, making eavesdropping and brute-force credential cracking on the wireless network computationally impossible – protecting sensitive training, financial, and administrative data end-to-end (Wi-Fi + MACsec-secured wired uplink).
- Wi-Fi CSI Sensing, allowing the same APs to double as presence/motion sensors for smart building-management integration (lighting, HVAC, room-utilization, dormitory occupancy) at no additional hardware cost – an optional value-add for [Client]'s hostel and classroom facilities.
All access points and site locations will be pre-simulated using enterprise-grade wireless planning software prior to installation, and a full coverage heat-map simulation report will be submitted for [Client]'s review and approval prior to physical deployment, as required.

#### 3.3.7  Network Security Architecture (Diagram 7 of 8 relevance – integrated with Data Center Diagram)

Security is layered end-to-end using Huawei USG6615F Next-Generation Firewalls (deployed as a high-availability active-active pair at HQ) and USG6525F firewall appliances at each RTC. All inter-site WAN traffic is encrypted (minimum AES-256) via the SD-WAN overlay; wired access from switch to AP/server is protected with MACsec; and wireless access is additionally protected by Wi-Fi Shield. Firewalls enforce stateful inspection, NAT, VPN termination, application-aware filtering, and network segmentation, and are centrally managed and monitored through iMaster NCE-Campus – with security event logs, alerts, and policy management hosted locally at HQ, requiring no mandatory cloud subscription.

#### 3.3.8  Data Centre and Server Network Architecture (Diagram 7 of 8 – Mandatory)

[Client] HQ functions as the primary data-centre and network-control hub. The stacked CloudEngine S6750-S core switches provide high-speed, redundant connectivity to server and storage infrastructure, with the NGFW pair providing perimeter protection and the iMaster NCE-Campus Network Management System attached directly at the core for unified visibility and control of the entire estate – satisfying the requirement for a centralized data-centre network design integrated with core switching, firewall placement, and network-management-system placement.

#### 3.3.9  Network Management and Monitoring Architecture (Diagram 8 of 8 – Mandatory)

A single instance of Huawei iMaster NCE-Campus, hosted locally at [Client] HQ under a permanent/perpetual licensing model, provides unified configuration management, performance monitoring, fault detection, traffic monitoring, security monitoring, and analytics across every managed device – SD-WAN routers, core/aggregation/access switches, firewalls, and wireless controllers/APs – at HQ and all 8 RTCs from one pane of glass. This directly satisfies [Client]'s explicit requirement for “LAN-WAN unified management, a set of software to manage SD-WAN, switches, and routers,” with a permanent license to avoid interruption from untimely renewal.
