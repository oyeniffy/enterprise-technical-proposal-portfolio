 FORM TECH-3 Comments and Suggestions on the Terms of Reference and on Counterpart Staff and

     A. On the Terms of Reference
Task Systems Limited has reviewed the Terms of Reference (Section 5 of the RFP) in detail and finds the scope, the nine-
line-item product list, and the one-month delivery timeline to be clear, well-specified, and achievable given TSL's existing
familiarity with the [Client] tenant. Because TSL is the incumbent provider, no discovery phase is required to understand the
current licensing baseline, this materially de-risks the compressed timeline relative to a new vendor mobilizing from a
standing start.

TSL proposes one refinement for [Client]'s consideration, which does not alter the license scope or product list defined in the
Terms of Reference: bundling the renewal with a focused enablement package covering Microsoft 365 Copilot readiness
assessment, an Azure security baseline review (identity, Microsoft Defender, and core security policies), a Power BI and
Microsoft Fabric readiness assessment, and structured administrator knowledge transfer. This is offered so that the renewal
cycle also leaves [Client]'s environment measurably more secure and better prepared for near-term analytics and AI adoption,
without extending the four-week delivery window or introducing any product not already reflected in the Terms of
Reference's license table.

TSL has deliberately excluded from this Proposal any third-party platform integration, IT service management tooling, or
premium licensing upgrade (such as M365 E5 or Microsoft 365 Copilot seat licenses) that would fall outside the renewal
scope and one-month timeline set out in the Terms of Reference. Where [Client] wishes to explore such initiatives in a future
phase, TSL would be glad to submit a separate proposal at that time.

     B. On Counterpart Staff and Facilities
To support delivery within the four-week window, TSL requests that [Client] nominate a single IT point of contact
empowered to coordinate tenant access, admin-console sign-off, and user communications for the duration of the
engagement. This counterpart role does not require a full-time secondment, an estimated two to four hours per week
of availability across the engagement period should be sufficient to keep the work plan at Form TECH-8 on schedule.

No dedicated office space, equipment, or facilities are required from [Client]. All work will be performed remotely against
the existing [Client] Microsoft 365 and Azure tenants, with on-site visits arranged as needed for knowledge-transfer and
administrator training sessions under the Professional Services scope priced at Form FIN-3.

         AI Readiness (Microsoft 365 Copilot)
AI adoption remains a medium-term priority for [Client], but this Proposal treats it as a readiness exercise rather than a
licensing commitment, no Copilot seat licenses are included in the commercial scope at Form FIN-3. The Microsoft 365
Copilot Readiness, Configuration & Enablement work stream will:

   i.     Assess the current tenant configuration, data governance posture, and licensing eligibility against Microsoft's
          Copilot prerequisites

   ii.    Identify any data labeling, permissions, or content hygiene gaps that should be closed before [Client] activates Copilot
          licenses in a future cycle

  iii.    Produce a short, actionable roadmap for a phased Copilot rollout by department and use case that [Client] can budget
          for and execute independently or with TSL support in a subsequent engagement

This keeps [Client]'s near-term investment focused on license continuity and security, while ensuring the organization is not
starting from zero when it is ready to move on AI adoption.
