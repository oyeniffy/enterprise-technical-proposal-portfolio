## Our Priority Core & Compliance Strategy

To secure your full license and operational success, Task Systems recommends a "Best-in-Class" dual-platform
approach. We focus on Finacle for operational agility and IBM Safer Payments for non-negotiable CBN regulatory
alignment.

### 1. The Core Engine: Finacle Islamic Banking Suite

We prioritize Finacle as the core banking application (CBA) to ensure your products are Sharia-compliant from day
one.

   Native Sharia Support: Built-in modules for Murabaha, Mudarabah, Ijarah, and Sukuk, eliminating the need for
    risky, expensive custom workarounds.
   Profit-Sharing Logic: Automated management of profit-distribution ratios and investment pools, ensuring total
    transparency for stakeholders.
   Scalable API Architecture: A digital-first engine that connects seamlessly to the Nigerian NIBSS and Fintech
    ecosystem.

### 2. The Regulatory Shield: IBM Safer Payments (AML/CFT/CPF)

In direct response to the CBN Baseline Standards, we deploy IBM Safer Payments as the primary solution for financial
crime prevention.

   Real-Time Detection: Unlike legacy systems, this AI-driven platform detects suspicious transactions in
    milliseconds, stopping financial crime before the value leaves the bank.
   CBN Circular Alignment: Directly addresses the core tenets of the CBN mandate: enhanced compliance reporting
    and the integration of emerging technologies.
   By deploying this best-in-class solution, [Client] will strengthen regulatory trust and operational efficiency through
    up to 77% reduction in false positives, enabling focus on high-risk threats
